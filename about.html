About Digital Electronics

Philadelphia's trusted electronics partner since 2010

Your Trusted Tech Partner in Philadelphia

Digital Electronics has been serving the Philadelphia community since 2010, providing residents and businesses with the latest consumer electronics, expert technical services, and unmatched customer support.

Located in the heart of Kensington, we are proud to be a neighborhood institution that has grown from a small repair shop into one of the most trusted electronics retailers in the greater Philadelphia area.

Our team of certified technicians and knowledgeable sales staff are passionate about technology and committed to helping every customer find the right solution for their needs and budget.
